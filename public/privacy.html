Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 • Atlas Broadcast / YouApp TV

1. Overview & Commitment

At Atlas Broadcast and YouApp TV ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Eduardo Rojo, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our broadcast automation and social syndication platform accesses, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect your social media accounts (such as TikTok, Meta, YouTube) to the Service.

2. What Atlas Broadcast Does

Atlas Broadcast is a suite designed for authorized media creators, broadcasters, and commercial partners to streamline video publication and multi-channel live feed distribution. Through official developer integrations, users can schedule, draft, and publish video content directly to their own connected social channels without sharing their login credentials.

3. Information We Collect Through APIs

Atlas Broadcast operates strictly on the principle of minimal data access. When you authorize the application through TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, we receive:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Your platform Open ID, public display name, and avatar URL to identify your account within the Atlas Broadcast control console.
  • OAuth Access & Refresh Tokens: Cryptographically generated security tokens provided by TikTok to perform authorized actions on your behalf. We NEVER receive or store your password.
  • Publishing Scopes (video.upload, video.publish): Permissions granted exclusively to upload video files and initiate publishing or draft creation upon your command.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information collected via TikTok APIs is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your connected channel identity in your personal Atlas Broadcast dashboard.
  • Uploading and publishing videos and captions that you explicitly schedule or initiate.
  • Checking the status of submitted video posts to confirm successful distribution.

We DO NOT:

  • Sell, lease, or monetize your personal or social profile data.
  • Share your data with third parties or data brokers.
  • Access your private messages, contacts, or financial details.
  • Publish any content without your explicit instruction.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All OAuth tokens and API secrets are stored using secure encryption in restricted credential stores. Access is protected by strict permission policies and transport encryption (TLS/HTTPS). We retain authentication tokens only for as long as your account remains connected to the Service.

6. Data Deletion & Revocation of Access

You retain full control over your data and connected accounts at all times:

  • Revoke from Third-Party Settings: You can revoke Atlas Broadcast permissions at any time directly in your TikTok application under Settings & Privacy → Security & Permissions → Apps and services.
  • Disconnect from Atlas: You can disconnect your accounts at any time inside the Atlas Broadcast Accounts dashboard, which immediately invalidates and purges stored access tokens.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may request complete erasure of any associated profile logs by emailing support@youapptv.com. Requests are fulfilled within 48 hours.

7. Compliance with TikTok API Terms

Our use of information received from TikTok APIs adheres to the TikTok Developer Terms of Service. We continuously maintain compliance with platform policies and security reviews.
